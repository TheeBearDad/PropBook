      Import trades

      Easiest: save CSVs into your TradesCSV folder. Each account has its own subfolder there; files dropped straight into TradesCSV go to the account you're viewing.

      Most futures prop firms (Lucid, Apex, Tradeify, Take Profit Trader, My Funded Futures and others) run on Tradovate. Export the account's closed trades there and drop the file here. Trades already in your journal are skipped. A CSV exported from this journal works too.

      1. Log in at trader.tradovate.com with that firm's credentials and pick the account.
      2. Open Account Reports, then the Performance tab.
      3. Pick your date range and download the CSV.
      4. NinjaTrader users: Trade Performance → Trades grid → right-click → Export.
