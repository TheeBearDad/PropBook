PROPBOOK - desktop app for macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later
===========================================================
Runs natively on both Apple Silicon (M1-M4) and Intel Macs.

INSTALL
  1. Unzip the download (Safari usually does this for you).
  2. Drag "PropBook.app" into your Applications folder.

FIRST LAUNCH
  The app isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer, so macOS
  asks you to confirm it once:

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later:
    1. Double-click PropBook. macOS says it "could not verify" the app.
       Click Done.
    2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
       "PropBook was blocked" message and click "Open Anyway".
    3. Enter your password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

  macOS 14 (Sonoma) and earlier:
    Control-click (or right-click) PropBook in Applications, choose
    Open, then click Open in the box that appears.

  After that it opens normally with a double-click.
  When it asks to access your Documents folder, click Allow: that's where
  your journal is saved.

  Only if macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened": open Terminal
  and paste this line, then open the app again:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/PropBook.app"

USING IT
  PropBook opens in its own window. Close the window and the app quits.
  It draws its window with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave (whichever
  you have). With none of those installed it opens in a Safari tab instead.

  Tip: drag PropBook from Applications onto the Dock to keep it handy.

ACCOUNTS
  Each prop firm account gets its own tab across the top (Lucid, Apex,
  Tradeify, Topstep and so on). Each tab has its own balance, max loss limit,
  profit target, calendar and stats. "All accounts" combines them and lists
  every account's room to its loss limit.

  - Add an account with the "+" tab. With the browser extension installed,
    opening a new account's dashboard shows "New account found" at the top of
    the journal: click Add account, link it to one you already have, or
    Ignore. Nothing is added without your click.
  - "Accounts" edits the selected account. Tick "Keep these limits" if you
    don't want the extension to change its loss limit or target. Tick "Hide
    this account" for accounts that are closed, failed or paid out; their
    trades stay. If the same account ever shows up twice, use "Merge it into".
  - When you log a trade, pick which account it belongs to.
  - Loss limit type: "Trailing" moves up as the account grows (Lucid, Apex,
    Tradeify...) and the extension keeps it current. "Static" is fixed from
    the starting balance and never moves (Vest Markets, and any plan with a
    fixed drawdown). Add a daily loss limit to see today's room on the meter.

SETUPS
  Pick one or more setups on every trade. Add your own with "Add setup" in the
  trade form, or open "Edit setups" to rename or delete them, hide presets you
  never use, and save combinations you use often (one click picks all of
  their setups). "P&L by setup" shows each setup, or each combination.

NEWS
  The News tab (next to Journal and Notes) puts your weekend reading in one
  place. It needs an internet connection.
  - Headlines: the latest stories from FinancialJuice, MarketWatch, CNBC,
    Yahoo Finance, Google News, the Federal Reserve and more, newest first.
    Press Refresh, or set "Auto-check" to look for new ones every 5, 15 or
    30 minutes while PropBook is open. Click a headline to open it in your
    browser. Data headlines like "Actual 0.4% (Forecast 0.3%)" get a green
    or red tag. "Sources" lets you turn sites on or off, add any site's RSS
    feed, and see which ones didn't load.
  - Red Folder: this week's economic calendar from ForexFactory for the US,
    Europe, the UK, Japan, Canada, China, Australia, New Zealand and
    Switzerland, with forecast and previous values and a countdown to the
    next big one. Folders are ranked for US futures traders: red = US
    high-impact events, orange = other countries' high-impact events and
    US medium ones, yellow = minor. Switch to "Folders: ForexFactory's" to
    use their own ratings. Click any folder to re-rank that event (for
    example, make CAD PPI red); PropBook remembers it. Use the country
    buttons to choose which countries to show. ForexFactory's free file
    doesn't include the released numbers, so "Show TradingView calendar"
    shows the actual numbers for the same countries.
  - Colors: "stock futures" colors results by what usually helps (green) or
    hurts (red) NQ and ES. Hot inflation counts as bad, for example. "US
    dollar" colors them the way ForexFactory does. Either way it's a rule of
    thumb, not a prediction.
  - Charts and Heatmap: TradingView's free embedded chart (NQ, ES, YM, RTY,
    gold, oil and more, or any symbol) and the S&P 500 / Nasdaq heatmap.
    Futures in these may be delayed.
  - The buttons at the top open FinancialJuice, ForexFactory, Unusual
    Whales, TradingView, MarketWatch and Google News in your browser.

NOTES
  Switch between "Journal" and "Notes" at the top of PropBook. Notes is a
  notepad for game plans, rules, end-of-day reviews or anything else. Click
  "+ New note", give it a title, and write. The toolbar has headings,
  bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, text color, highlight, checkbox
  lists, bulleted and numbered lists, indents, a divider line, today's date
  and links. Click a checkbox to tick it. Notes save as you type, and are
  kept in your journal file with everything else. Pin a note to keep it at
  the top of the list, and use the search box to find one.
  Shortcuts: Cmd+B bold, Cmd+I italic, Cmd+U underline, Cmd+K link,
  Tab / Shift+Tab to indent a list item, Cmd+Enter to tick a checkbox.

RISK : REWARD
  Each trade has a Target price, a Risk ($) and a Planned risk : reward.
  Fill in your entry, stop and target, and PropBook works out the risk and
  the R:R for you. You can also type them yourself, for example 1:2 or just
  2. Risk lets PropBook show each trade's result in R (net P&L divided by
  the risk), so a $120 win on $40 of risk is +3R. The R column in the trade
  list shows both. The "Avg R:R" and "Avg result" tiles show the month's
  averages, plus your all-time average R:R.

TIME IN THE TRADE
  Enter the entry and exit times and the time in trade fills in. Add how long
  the trade was in profit and how long it was in the red; the bar under the
  fields shows the split. Type times as 6:30 (6 min 30 sec), 6m 30s or
  1:05:00. Tradovate imports fill in the time in trade for you. "Time in
  trade" under What's working shows how your hold time affects your results.

UPGRADING FROM "TRADE JOURNAL"
  PropBook used to be called Trade Journal. The first time PropBook opens, it
  moves your Documents/Trade Journal folder to Documents/PropBook, trades,
  notes and settings included. You can delete the old Trade Journal program.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  Documents/PropBook/
     journal-data.json   all your trades, journal notes and account settings
     screenshots/        chart screenshots you attach to trades
     exports/            CSV files from the "Export CSV" button
     TradesCSV/          drop trade CSVs here to import them automatically
     backups/            an automatic copy each day you open the app (last 30 kept)

  Nothing is sent anywhere; it all stays on your Mac. The folder works the
  same on Windows, so you can copy it between computers.

IMPORTING TRADES (automatic)
  Most futures prop firms (Lucid, Apex, Tradeify, Take Profit Trader,
  My Funded Futures...) run on Tradovate:
  1. Log in at trader.tradovate.com with that firm's credentials.
  2. Account Reports > Performance tab > choose dates > download the CSV.
  3. Put the CSV in that account's folder:
        Documents/PropBook/TradesCSV/<Firm - Account name>/
     The app makes one folder per account. A CSV dropped straight into
     TradesCSV goes to whichever account tab you have open.

  While the app is open it imports new files within a few seconds, refreshes,
  and moves the file to TradesCSV/Imported. Files dropped in while the app is
  closed are imported next time you open it. A CSV that isn't a trade report
  goes to TradesCSV/Not imported, and the app tells you why.
  Trades already in the journal are skipped, so overlapping dates are fine.

  You can also use "Import trades" and choose the account there.

BROWSER EXTENSION (Chrome or Brave) - syncs from your prop firm dashboards
  PropBook Sync is a browser extension. When you open an
  account page on your firm's dashboard, it reads the balance, max loss limit
  / liquidation threshold, profit target, and (where the dashboard has them)
  the P&L calendar and trade history, and sends them to the matching account
  in the app. A new account it hasn't seen before gets its own tab.

  Built-in sites: Lucid, Apex, Tradeify, Topstep, My Funded Futures,
  Take Profit Trader, Bulenox, Earn2Trade, TradeDay, Elite Trader Funding.
  Any other firm: open its dashboard, click the extension icon, then
  "Read this site too".

  Tradovate CSV exports you download are saved to a "PropBook" folder in
  Downloads, and the app imports them into the account tab you have open.
  (The first time, macOS asks whether PropBook may access Downloads:
  click Allow.)

  Install (one time):
  1. Open PropBook once. It puts the extension in
        Documents/PropBook/PropBook Sync
     (Settings > Browser extension > "Open extension folder" shows it).
  2. Chrome: go to  chrome://extensions   Brave: go to  brave://extensions
  3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
  4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose that "PropBook Sync" folder.
     Load it from there and nowhere else: that's the copy PropBook keeps
     up to date.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin "PropBook Sync".
  Coming from "Trade Journal Sync"? Remove that one from the extensions
  page first, then load PropBook Sync as above.

  Pair it with the app (one time):
  1. In the app, open "Settings". Copy the pairing key under "Browser extension".
  2. Click the extension's icon, paste the key, click Save.

  Dashboards differ from firm to firm. If an account's numbers don't come
  through: open that account's page, click the extension icon, then
  "Copy page text (for troubleshooting)", and include it when you report the
  problem so the reader can be adjusted for that firm.

VEST MARKETS (crypto perps)
  Vest has no API, so trades come in by CSV export:
  1. Add the account: firm "Vest Markets" and a name with its size, such as
     "25K 1-Step". The journal fills in a static 6% loss limit (check it
     against your plan) and you add the profit target and daily loss limit.
  2. In Vest, open Positions History and click Export CSV. Vest exports the
     account selected at the top, so export each account separately.
  3. Put the file in that account's TradesCSV folder (or use Import trades).
  To log a perp trade by hand, pick "Other symbol" as the contract, type the
  symbol (BTC-PERP) and the size in coins; P&L is size x price change.
  The extension also reads balances from Vest's site when it can. If the
  numbers or the CSV columns don't come through, use the extension's
  "Copy page text" or send a sample export along with the problem report.

LIGHT AND DARK MODE
  The sun/moon button at the top switches between light and dark. Settings >
  Appearance can also set it to match your computer.

UPDATES
  PropBook checks for a new version when it opens and every few hours. When
  one is out, a bar at the top says so: click "Update now" and PropBook
  downloads it, checks it, and restarts on the new version. Your journal is
  untouched. Turn on "Install updates automatically" under Settings >
  Updates and startup to skip the click. Updates are signed, and PropBook refuses any
  download whose signature or checksum doesn't match.
  The browser extension updates with the app: PropBook refreshes the files in
  the PropBook Sync folder and the extension reloads itself.
  Automatic updates need PropBook in your Applications folder. If it runs
  from Downloads, macOS blocks it from replacing itself.

OPEN AT LOGIN
  To have PropBook open when you log in, tick "Open PropBook when I sign in
  to this computer" under Settings > Updates and startup. PropBook needs to
  be in your Applications folder for this. Untick it to turn it off.

SHARING PROPBOOK
  The download contains no personal data. Your journal lives only in the
  Documents/PropBook folder on your own computer. To give PropBook
  to someone, send them the zip you downloaded (not your Documents folder):
  they start with an empty journal, set up their own accounts, and pair the
  extension with their own key.

CHANGING THE ICON
  Window icon: put any square picture named  icon.png  in
  Documents/PropBook, then reopen the app. Delete it to go back to the
  candlestick icon.
  App icon in Finder and the Dock: select PropBook in Applications,
  press Cmd-I, and drag a picture onto the small icon at the top left.
