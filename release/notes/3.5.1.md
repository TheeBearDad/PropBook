### Red Folder: every major country
- **All the major economies.** The US, Europe, the UK, Japan, Canada, China, Australia, New Zealand and Switzerland are on by default. Tap the country buttons to choose, or use **All** / **US only**.
- **Folders ranked for US futures traders:**
  - 🟥 **Red** means high impact on US futures: the US red folders.
  - 🟧 **Orange** is less important: other countries' red folders and US orange ones.
  - 🟨 **Yellow** is minor.
  - Prefer ForexFactory's own ratings? Pick **Folders: ForexFactory's**.
- **Re-rank any event.** Click its folder to cycle red → orange → yellow. For example, click CAD PPI to always show it as red. PropBook remembers the change, and a small dot marks events you've re-ranked.
- The default view is now **Red + orange**, so other countries' big releases show up next to the US ones.
- Other countries' numbers are colored for their own currency. US numbers still follow the stock-futures or US-dollar setting.
- The TradingView calendar (for released numbers) now follows the countries you pick.

### Updating
Click **Update now** on the bar at the top of PropBook, or go to **Settings → Check for updates**.
