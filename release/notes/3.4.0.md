### New: Notes
Switch between **Journal** and **Notes** at the top of PropBook.

- A notepad for game plans, rules, end-of-day reviews or anything else you want to keep next to your trades.
- **Formatting toolbar:** Normal / Heading / Subheading / Quote, **bold**, *italic*, underline, ~~strikethrough~~, text color, highlight, checkbox lists, bulleted and numbered lists, indents, a divider line, today's date and links.
- **Checkboxes:** click one to tick it. The note shows how many are checked.
- **Saves as you type**, in the same journal file as your trades. Notes come along when you copy your PropBook folder to a new computer.
- **List of notes** with search, the newest first, and **Pin** to keep important ones at the top. Notes you leave empty are cleaned up on their own.
- Pasting from websites or other apps keeps the text and basic formatting and drops anything unsafe.
- Works on small screens too: the list and the open note each take the full screen.
- Shortcuts: Ctrl+B / I / U, Ctrl+K for a link, Tab to indent a list item, Ctrl+Enter to tick a checkbox.

### Updating
Click **Update now** on the bar at the top of PropBook, or go to **Settings → Check for updates**.
