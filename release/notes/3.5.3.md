### Fixed
- **Updating no longer leaves the old window open.** PropBook now closes its window completely, including when Edge has quietly moved it to another of its processes, and only then opens the new version. Updating from an older version also cleans up the old window it left behind.

### New in News
- **Red market-moving headlines.** Stories a source marks as market moving, like FinancialJuice's red ones, now show in red with a **MARKET MOVING** label.
- **Tags** from the source (USD, US Indexes, JPY, NVDA…) show under each headline.
- **Pictures.** A chart or photo that comes with a story appears with it. Click it to see it full size.
- **Market moving only** filter.
- **Your own highlight words.** In **News → Sources**, words like *intervention* or *Powell* turn matching headlines red with a **WATCH** label. It starts with *breaking, intervention, emergency, unscheduled*.
- Multi-line updates such as FinancialJuice's **MOC Imbalance** keep their lines.
- Data headlines with no forecast (*"Forecast -"*) are now compared with the previous number.
