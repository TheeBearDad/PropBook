### New: pictures in Notes
- **Paste a screenshot** (Ctrl+V, for example right after Win+Shift+S), **drag a picture in**, or use the new **picture button** on the toolbar. You can add several at once.
- **Click a picture** to make it Small, Medium, Large or Full width, **View** it full size, or **Remove** it. Delete works too, and Ctrl+Z brings it back. Double-click opens the full-size view.
- Pictures are saved in `Documents/PropBook/screenshots`, next to your trade screenshots, so they don't bloat your journal file. Very large screenshots are shrunk to 2400 pixels wide.
- The notes list shows how many pictures each note has.
- Text pasted from websites keeps its formatting, but pictures from other sites aren't brought along, so a note never loads anything from the internet.
