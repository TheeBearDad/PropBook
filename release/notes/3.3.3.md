### New
- **Risk : reward on every trade.** The trade form has **Target price**, **Risk ($)** and **Planned risk : reward**. Enter your entry, stop and target, and PropBook fills in the risk and R:R. You can also type your own, for example `1:2` or `2`. This works for trades you log by hand and for trades you journal from your firm's dashboard.
- **Result in R.** With a risk set, each trade shows its result in R (net P&L ÷ risk), so a $120 win on $40 of risk is **+3R**.
- **R column** in the trade list shows the result and the planned R:R.
- **Avg R:R** and **Avg result** tiles show the month's averages, plus your all-time average R:R.
- Target, risk and R:R are included in Export CSV and in PropBook CSV imports.
