### Changed
- **New Settings window.** Settings now has its own button next to **Accounts**. It holds appearance, commissions, the browser extension, updates and startup, and your data folders. **Accounts** is just for your accounts.
- Commission changes save as soon as you type them.
- PropBook Sync 2.3.1: the popup now sends you to **Settings** for the pairing key. The extension updates itself from the PropBook Sync folder.

### Updating
If you have 3.3.0 or 3.3.1, click **Update now** on the bar at the top of PropBook. If you're on anything older, download this version below.

| Download | For |
|---|---|
| `PropBook-3.3.2-Windows.zip` | Windows 10/11 |
| `PropBook-3.3.2-macOS.zip` | macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) |
