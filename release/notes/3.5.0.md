### New: News tab
Click **News**, next to Journal and Notes, to get your weekend reading in one place.

- **Headlines.** The latest stories from FinancialJuice, MarketWatch, CNBC, Yahoo Finance, Google News, the Federal Reserve and more, merged into one list and grouped by day.
  - Press **Refresh**, or set **Auto-check** to look for new stories every 5, 15 or 30 minutes while PropBook is open.
  - New stories since you last looked are marked.
  - Search, filter by source, and click a headline to open it in your browser.
  - Data headlines such as *"US CPI Actual 0.4% (Forecast 0.3%)"* get a green or red tag.
  - **Sources** lets you turn sites on or off, add any site's RSS feed, and see which ones didn't load and why.
- **Red Folder.** This week's ForexFactory calendar.
  - Red-folder events by default, with forecast and previous values, a ▲/▼ showing which way the forecast leans, and a countdown to the next release.
  - Filters for orange folders and other currencies.
  - ForexFactory's free weekly file doesn't include the released numbers, so **Show TradingView calendar** brings up TradingView's calendar with the actual numbers.
- **Green / red.** Choose **stock futures**, which shows what usually helps or hurts NQ and ES (hot inflation counts as bad), or **US dollar**, which matches ForexFactory's colors. It's a rule of thumb, not a prediction.
- **Charts.** TradingView's free embedded chart for NQ, ES, YM, RTY, gold, oil, the dollar index, VIX, the 10-year yield or any symbol. Futures prices there can be delayed.
- **Heatmap.** TradingView's S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow 30 or all-US heatmap.
- Buttons at the top open **FinancialJuice, ForexFactory, Unusual Whales, TradingView, MarketWatch and Google News** in your browser. Those sites don't allow other apps to show their pages.

PropBook only reads the public feeds and files these sites publish for readers. It saves what it last loaded, so the tab still shows something when you're offline.
